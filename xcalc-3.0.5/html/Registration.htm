Registering XCALC

Don't panic. There is NO money involved – XCALC is GNU software.

But if you continue to use XCALC, these are your terms:

  • You must inform me that you're an XCALC user, either by emailing me, or by sending me a postcard.
  • XCALC is spiceware: I like cooking, so please consider sending me some nice spices! There are so many lovely blends out there, and I would like to sample some of your local specialities. Anything goes!
  • And (and now I’m widening the territory from where I ask for this): if you can find a jar of Vegemite, or a jar of Master Foods' Hot English Mustard in your shop, it would be most appreciated. I can’t get hold of it in Norway, and I love it (the Vegemite is also my daughter’s favourite)… Please send me some! My address is here.
